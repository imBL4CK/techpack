# <Color id="blue">Circuits</Color>

Circuits are the heart of electrical machines; it is through this component that the machine is able to perform its function.

Circuits feature a simple electrical conduction system that may suffice for some machines; however, in cases where higher current is required, it may be necessary to use conduction coils or cables.

In general, circuits correspond to specific technology levels, but they can also be derived for a particular application, such as the spiritual.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="techpack:basic_circuit"/>

  ### <Color id="light_purple">Basic Circuit</Color>
</Row>
The most basic electrical circuit capable of conducting and managing electric currents. It also has the capability to be programmed to perform the function of a machine, making it the most cost-effective method for equipping a machine.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="pneumaticcraft:printed_circuit_board"/>

  ### <Color id="light_purple">Finished PCB</Color>
</Row>
This is an improved version of the original <ItemLink id="techpack:basic_circuit" /> concept. Its base is constructed from gold wires, HDPE, and silicon—materials that alone make it superior to the standard Basic Circuit board.

However, the improvements don't stop there. During the final manufacturing stage, it is fitted with internal resistors, diodes, transistors, and capacitors, resulting in a much more robust version than the basic circuit. The advancements are so significant that these circuits are used in the recipes for various AE2 components.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="techpack:spiritual_circuit"/>

  ### <Color id="light_purple">Spiritual Circuit</Color>
</Row>
A fork of the <ItemLink id="pneumaticcraft:printed_circuit_board" />, designed specifically to conduct enchantment and spiritual energies. 

Created from the unassembled PCB used in the final stage of the <ItemLink id="pneumaticcraft:printed_circuit_board" />, it is equipped with various electronic components crafted from magical materials, making it a perfect circuit for channeling magical and spiritual energy.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="techpack:sophisticated_circuit"/>

  ### <Color id="light_purple">Sophisticated Circuit</Color>
</Row>
The ideal version of a complete circuit. It features all the improvements of the <ItemLink id="pneumaticcraft:printed_circuit_board" />, along with significant additions.

The board is constructed from stainless steel and polytetrafluoroethylene sheets, and is equipped with a more robust capacitor and a more efficient electron tube. In the final stage, it is sealed with ether gas, which keeps the components constantly cooled.

Its primary uses are in the production of high-end equipment, such as the <ItemLink id="industrialforegoing:ore_laser_base" />.
