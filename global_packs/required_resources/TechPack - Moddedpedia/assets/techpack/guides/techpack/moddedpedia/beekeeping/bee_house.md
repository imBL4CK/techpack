# <Color id="blue">Bee House</Color>
</Row>
A bee house is a resting place for bees and also a site where they can reproduce. 

When a drone and a queen of the same species are placed inside a bee house, there is a chance they will produce offspring of the same species.

* <Color id="red">Bees can lose lifespan while inside the bee house</Color>, so it is a good idea to periodically check their health.

# <Color id="blue">Genetic Mutation</Color>

Some bees are capable of producing offspring adapted to specific conditions, these offspring are called mutants.

* In general, mutant bees <Color id="red">cannot be obtained except through the Mutation Station</Color>.
* To <Color id="light_purple">enable genetic mutation</Color>, you need to press the <Color id="green">"toggle genetic mutation"</Color> button inside a bee house.
