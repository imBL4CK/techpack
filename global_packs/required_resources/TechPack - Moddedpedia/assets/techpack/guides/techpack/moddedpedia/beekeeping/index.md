# Beekeeping

# <Color id="blue">Beekeeping</Color>
Beekeeping is one of the mechanics added in the modpack. This activity consists of taking care of bees so that they produce honeycombs that can be processed into different products.

* <Color id="red">It is important to remember that each bee has its own characteristics</Color>, and depending on climate, nearby blocks, and other factors, it can produce different honeycombs.

The basis of beekeeping is obtaining bees, which are divided into 2 classes: Drones and Queens. Drones are the most common class of bees, while queens are rarer to find. Inside a beehive or apiary, it is important that these two classes coexist.

* <Color id="light_purple">Queen bees are the rarest class</Color> because they are capable of producing offspring.

## <Color id="yellow">Honeycomb Production</Color>
To produce honeycombs, a <ItemLink id="techpack:apiary"/> is required. The apiary is a workstation that performs the task of generating a honeycomb when the recipe conditions are met—such as climate, nearby blocks, dimension, etc.

* <Color id="green">To learn more</Color>, visit the specific page about <ItemLink id="techpack:apiary"/>.

## <Color id="yellow">Bee Breeding</Color>
Bees have a limited lifespan, and they lose this lifespan over the course of their activities; however, it is possible to breed them. To produce offspring, it is necessary to place a queen and a drone in a <ItemLink id="techpack:bee_house"/> with a view of the sky.

* <Color id="green">To learn more</Color>, visit the specific page about <ItemLink id="techpack:bee_house"/>.

## <Color id="yellow">Bee Mutation</Color>
Some bees have the ability to produce offspring of a different species. The result of this mating is called mutant bees.

To produce mutant bees, you need to press the "toggle genetic mutation" button on a <ItemLink id="techpack:bee_house"/> and meet all the necessary conditions for mating to occur.
