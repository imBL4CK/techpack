# Electrical Machine

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="techpack:electric_metal_former"/>

  # <Color id="blue">Metal Former</Color>
</Row>

A Metal Former is an electric machine similar to a compressor, sharing comparable functionality—but differing in its end use. Metal formers utilize three pistons operating in different directions, allowing them to shape less dense items rather than simply pressing them in a single direction.

* It interacts with wrenches, and its slots can be configured for item input and output.
* Energy consumption varies between recipes, so it is worth checking.

## <Color id="yellow">Upgrades and Integral Augments</Color>
It features slots for upgrades as well as for Integral Augments.

**<Color id="light_purple">Upgrades</Color>**: These alter one of the machine's attributes at the cost of another—for instance, reducing the time required for a recipe while increasing energy consumption. Upgrades are installed in the slot that resembles a circuit.

**<Color id="light_purple">Integral Augments</Color>**: These scale a machine based on its base attributes without reducing any other attribute. Integral Augments also unlock new recipes; if an Integral Augment is of a higher tier than the minimum, it inherits the recipes from the previous tier.
