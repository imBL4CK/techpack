# Synthetic Material

# <Color id="blue">Plastics</Color>

Plastics are flexible synthetic materials composed of long chains of organic polymers that can be easily molded under heat and pressure.

Plastics are indispensable in an industrial context, as they can lower costs for many resources—for instance, by serving as electrical insulators instead of rubber. They can also be used as adhesives when heated into a fluid state, among various other applications.

However, there are many varieties of plastics; while they share common uses, they can also possess specific characteristics—such as polytetrafluoroethylene, which is chemically resistant.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="pneumaticcraft:plastic"/>

  ### <Color id="light_purple">Polyethylene</Color>
</Row>
One of the most common polymers, derived directly from petroleum. Polyethylene can be easily obtained by superheating ethylene in a thermopneumatic processing plant at temperatures above 100 degrees.

One of its most common uses is as an electrical insulator, but it can be processed into <ItemLink id="techpack:hdpe_sheet" />, one of the most widely used materials in crafting.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="techpack:bio_plastic_sheet"/>

  ### <Color id="light_purple">Polyurethane</Color>
</Row>
Obtained by superheating biodiesel and <ItemLink id="minecraft:charcoal" /> at temperatures above 100 degrees. It shares the same use as an electrical insulator but does not have a concentrated version. It is essentially a cheaper form of polyethylene made from organic materials.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="techpack:ptfe_sheet"/>

  ### <Color id="light_purple">Polytetrafluoroethylene</Color>
</Row>
Obtained through a sequence of chemical reactions. In addition to serving as an electrical insulator, it possesses a unique characteristic: chemical resistance. This property is highly useful for building machines that handle chemicals or require a more durable plastic.
