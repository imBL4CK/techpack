  # <Color id="blue">Fluid Cell</Color>
</Row>

Fluid cells are very interesting tools because they can hold up to 1,000 mB of fluid. At first glance, you might think they function just like a bucket, but that isn't quite the case.

Buckets can only carry fluid if exactly 1,000 mB of that fluid is present; fluid cells, however, can hold precise, partial amounts ranging from 1 to 1,000 mB.

In addition to this convenience, they also allow you to extract fluids from or insert them into blocks simply by right-clicking.
