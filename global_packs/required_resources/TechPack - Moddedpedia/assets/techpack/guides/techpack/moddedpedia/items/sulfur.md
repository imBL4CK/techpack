Sulfur is a nonmetallic chemical element. Its powder is important in the rubber recipe, a widely used and readily available component of TechPack.

## <Color id="light_purple">Sulfur can be found in various ways</Color>:
*   as crystals in caves on the Overworld, Mars, and the Moon
*   in loot chests
*   as geodes in the Nether and on Venus

Their geodes are large and contain a large amount of sulfur. Finding one can be a great help.
