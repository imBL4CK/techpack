# Machines, Generators and Devices

The modpack adds machines, generators, and devices that are used throughout the progression.

**<Color id="blue">Machines</Color>**, Come in various types, ranging from steam-powered units to those that consume electricity.

**<Color id="blue">Generators</Color>**, As the name implies, produce electricity from a fuel source—which could be an item, a fluid, and so on.

**<Color id="blue">Devices</Color>**, On the other hand, are a specific type of machine designed for a dedicated purpose; they do not necessarily require a power source to operate.
