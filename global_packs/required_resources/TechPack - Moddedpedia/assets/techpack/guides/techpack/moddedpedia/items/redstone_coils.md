# Synthetic Material

# <Color id="blue">Redstone Coils</Color>

Redstone coils are components used in the manufacture of tools and electrical machines. Each type of coil has a specific use for which it is best suited.

Coils are essentially <ItemLink id="create:polished_rose_quartz"/> wrapped with metal plates and redstone.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="techpack:redstone_reception_coil"/>

  ### <Color id="light_purple">Reception Coil</Color>
</Row>
These coils are specialized for receiving energy without loss. The gold used in their composition does not oxidize, making them highly durable and reliable in the long run.

However, gold is highly malleable when exposed to high temperatures, which is why it is used solely for receiving energy.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="techpack:redstone_transmission_coil"/>

  ### <Color id="light_purple">Transmission Coil</Color>
</Row>
These coils are the best when it comes to transmitting energy via high-current flows. The silver used in their manufacture allows for higher voltage levels.

However, silver is susceptible to oxidation, which is why it is not used for energy reception.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="techpack:redstone_conductance_coil"/>

  ### <Color id="light_purple">Conductance Coil</Color>
</Row>
These coils serve as an intermediary between energy reception and transfer. They are primarily used in the manufacture of portable batteries and high-capacity terminals.

Electrum is the material used in their production; it oxidizes less frequently than silver and withstands high temperatures better than gold, making it the best choice.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="techpack:redstone_spiritual_coil"/>

  ### <Color id="light_purple">Spiritual Coil</Color>
</Row>
Finally, there is a specific type of coil. Spiritual coils catalyze both electrical and spiritual energy and are strictly necessary for creating machines and gadgets that combine these properties.

The material used for these coils is soul-stained steel, a metal with spiritual energy-conducting properties.
