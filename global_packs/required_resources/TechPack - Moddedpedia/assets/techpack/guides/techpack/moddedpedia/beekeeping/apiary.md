# Beekeeping Workstation

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="techpack:apiary"/>

  # <Color id="blue">Apiary</Color>
</Row>
The apiary is a workstation used to produce combs from two bees: a queen and a drone.

The bees inside the apiary work to generate different types of combs depending on the conditions. Since <Color id="green">some bee species can produce more than one type of comb</Color>, it is important to carefully check the recipe requirements to avoid producing the wrong one.

While a bee attempts to produce a honeycomb, <Color id="light_purple">it may generate beeswax</Color>. Beeswax is a type of wax that can be used in the recipes for other items.

* <Color id="red">Bees can lose lifespan while inside the apiary</Color>, so it is a good idea to periodically check their health.
